# Contributing to Mule Create

Thanks for your interest in Mule! Here is how you can help during the alpha.

## Feedback and bug reports: very welcome

Please open an **Issue** on GitHub:

- **Bug report:** what you did, what you expected, what happened instead. The Mule version
  (the release name), your system, and if possible the scene file or a screenshot.
- **Idea or wish:** what you are trying to do and where Mule gets in your way.
- **Workflow with an AI:** what worked well or badly when handing a Sketch Brief to an AI.
  This is just as valuable as a bug report.

Questions, ideas to discuss, and things you made with Mule: **Discussions** on GitHub.
For anything that does not fit there, write to **mulecreate@gmail.com**.

## Code contributions: not accepted at the moment

Mule is developed by one person. During the alpha, **pull requests are not reviewed or merged**
and may be closed without comment. This is not about your work: it keeps the project focused,
and it keeps the licensing in one hand so that it can still be changed later.

If you have built something on top of Mule, tell us about it in an Issue instead. This rule may
be relaxed later; this file will say so.
